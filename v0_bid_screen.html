HOW THE SCREEN IS BUILT
1

Understand

Verified device data: grade, battery, faults, quantity.

2

Benchmark

What comparable devices actually cleared at, recently.

3

Recommend

A suggested bid from comparables, the buyer's own history, condition and demand.

4

Decide

Accept the suggestion or type another number.

DESIGN RULES

Suggest, never auto-bid

Bidding on a buyer's behalf would remove the judgement that makes price discovery real.

Always show the basis

A number with no reasoning gets ignored once, then distrusted.

Admit low confidence

On rare models or new buyers, say the data is thin instead of inventing precision.
